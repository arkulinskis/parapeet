One phrase. Ten moods.

Every language changes with who you're talking to. "Lend me your pen" to a colleague isn't what you'd say to your boss, to someone who's furious, or to the person you love. Parapeet teaches all ten.

How it works

Five minutes a day. No textbook, no grammar tables: real phrases for real situations at work and at home.

Swipe the moods

Each card is one phrase. Swipe sideways to hear it said to your boss, a client or your partner. Swipe up for the next one. The words that change are highlighted.

Play short lessons

Guess the mood, pick the meaning, build the sentence from word tiles, listen and choose, then say it out loud. Wrong answers come back at the end.

Catch Parapeet

Every right answer earns seeds. Keep your streak, hit your daily goal, collect badges. At 3,000 seeds, Parapeet is caught and your words are back.

Questions

Is Parapeet free?

Yes. You can play right away without an account; the sample phrases and every game feature are free. A free account unlocks more phrases and keeps your seeds, streak and phrasebook on every device.

Which languages can I learn?

French, Spanish and Italian: the same phrases in the same ten moods. Tap the little flag at the top to switch. Your seeds, streak and badges come with you; each language keeps its own lessons and saved phrases.

What level do I need?

Parapeet is for people who already know some of the language (around A2 to B2) and want to sound natural in different situations. Every card has the English on the back.

Which French, Spanish and Italian?

Standard, everyday language that works everywhere: French for France and Québec (the speech check listens for Canadian French), neutral Spanish for Spain and Latin America ("ustedes", no "vosotros"), and standard Italian with the formal "Lei" written with a capital L so you can spot it.

What does Parapeet do with my voice?

Record me keeps your recording in your browser only; it is never uploaded. Say it uses your browser's speech recognition, which in some browsers (like Chrome) is run by the browser maker online. Details are in Privacy & Terms.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Swipe the cards with your thumb, and add Parapeet to your home screen to open it like an app.

How is this different from other language apps?

Most apps teach you one way to say something. Parapeet teaches the same sentence in ten moods, so you learn tone, politeness and feeling, not just words.
