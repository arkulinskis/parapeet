One phrase. Ten moods.

French changes with who you're talking to. "Lend me your pen" to a colleague isn't what you'd say to your boss, to someone who's furious, or to the person you love. Parapeet teaches all ten.

Questions

Is Parapeet free?

Yes. You can play right away without an account; the sample phrases and every game feature are free. A free account unlocks more phrases and keeps your seeds, streak and phrasebook on every device.

What level of French do I need?

Parapeet is for people who already know some French (around A2 to B2) and want to sound natural in different situations. Every card has the English on the back.

Is it French from France or from Québec?

The phrases are standard, everyday French that works in both. The speech check listens for Canadian French and understands a French accent too.

What does Parapeet do with my voice?

Record me keeps your recording in your browser only; it is never uploaded. Say it uses your browser's speech recognition, which in some browsers (like Chrome) is run by the browser maker online. Details are in Privacy & Terms.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Swipe the cards with your thumb, and add Parapeet to your home screen to open it like an app.

How is this different from other language apps?

Most apps teach you one way to say something. Parapeet teaches the same sentence in ten moods, so you learn tone, politeness and feeling, not just words.
