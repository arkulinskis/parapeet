Languages

French, Spanish and Italian share one LineID per phrase and the English in FrenchSentences_translation. Spanish and Italian live in SpanishSentences and ItalianSentences (voices: VoiceFiles_spanish, VoiceFiles_italian). To translate: download the phrases, add rows with Lang es or it (same LineID, one column per mood), then upload the file. Only Spanish and Italian rows are written: a known LineID is updated (empty cells keep the old text), a new one is added. French and English rows in the file are skipped.

French library

The French phrases live in the FrenchSentences and FrenchSentences_translation tables (edit them in the Anvil editor). Availability: basic = everyone, free = any account, premium = customer_type "premium".
